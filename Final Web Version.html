Attendance Management Policy
Network Rail, v1.00, 23 July 2023. A quick reference — tap a topic to expand it. Always check the full document for exact wording.
This app is a personal record-keeping tool, intended to help you track your own attendance, leave, and shift information accurately so you can understand and stay compliant with company policy. It is not intended to help avoid or minimise genuine attendance management processes. It has no connection to your employer and is not an official company system — always refer to your line manager and the official policy documents for anything affecting your employment.
Your entries are private to you. They're saved only against your own use of this app and aren't visible to the person who built it, your colleagues, or your employer — nobody else can see what you log here.
